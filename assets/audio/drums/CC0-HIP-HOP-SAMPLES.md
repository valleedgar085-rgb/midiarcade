# Bundled CC0 Hip-Hop drum samples

Source repository: https://github.com/Boochi44/free-drum-samples

Bundled subsets:
- 02 Bounce: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat
- 03 Soulful Vintage: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat

The upstream repository states that all samples are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) and may be used, modified, and redistributed in commercial projects without attribution.

Some upstream samples were derived from CC0 TR-808 recordings by Edward Loveall. Attribution is not required, but this notice is retained for provenance.

These samples are used only for MIDI Arcade's offline audio preview. MIDI export remains MIDI data and does not embed the WAV files.
