PRIVACY POLICY

Music stays yours.

MIDI Arcade is designed as a local-first song creation and piano improvisation app. This policy explains what the app handles, where that information stays, and what happens when you choose to export a MIDI file.

The short version

MIDI Arcade does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, a crash-reporting SDK, or a developer-operated cloud service. Songs, MIDI performances, practice statistics, and settings are processed and stored on your device unless you explicitly export or share a MIDI file.

Information handled on your device

Music and practice data

To restore your session, MIDI Arcade saves app data in local application storage. This can include:

  • generated notes, arrangement sections, tempo, title, instruments, and mix settings;
  • mute, solo, lock, selected-track, guide, keyboard-range, and workflow settings;
  • a recorded piano take represented as MIDI notes, timing, duration, and velocity;
  • on-device feedback and practice statistics such as best score, session count, streak, and last practice date; and
  • the date and time when the local session was saved.

This information is used only for features including autosave, playback, editing, practice feedback, and MIDI export. MIDI Arcade does not transmit it to the developer.

MIDI devices and input

Hardware MIDI access begins only after you choose to find or connect a keyboard. The app may display a connected MIDI device's name and manufacturer. MIDI note-on, note-off, velocity, channel, and sustain-pedal messages are processed on the device so you can hear, record, and score a performance.

Device identifiers and names are not included in the saved session. MIDI Arcade records note events—not microphone audio—and does not access the microphone to create a take.

Information not collected by the developer

The current version does not collect or receive:

  • names, email addresses, account credentials, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment information;
  • generated songs, recorded MIDI takes, MIDI-device identity, or exported files;
  • analytics events, usage profiles, or cross-app tracking data; or
  • microphone recordings, photos, videos, or files from your media library.

The developer does not sell personal information or share app data for advertising.

Google Play and Android may independently provide the developer with aggregate or de-identified store performance, install/uninstall, rating, and Android Vitals crash or ANR diagnostics under Google's own terms. This platform reporting is not produced by an analytics or crash-reporting SDK inside MIDI Arcade and does not include your MIDI notes, song content, or exported files.

MIDI export and user-directed sharing

When you select Export, MIDI Arcade creates a Standard MIDI File on your device. On Android, the app writes a temporary .mid file to its private cache and opens the Android share/save sheet. You decide whether to save the file or send it to another app, device, or service.

MIDI Arcade does not upload the export to the developer. A destination you select may process the file under its own privacy policy. Temporary cache files may remain until Android removes them or you clear the app's storage. Copies saved or shared outside MIDI Arcade are controlled by you and are not removed when app storage is cleared.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local session data remains until it is replaced, cleared through Android's app-storage controls, or removed when the app is uninstalled. A recorded take can also be cleared from Jam Studio. Exported copies must be deleted from their destination by you.

The Android release configuration disables app backup and explicitly excludes app data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Exported copies saved elsewhere remain subject to the selected destination's backup behavior. The MIDI Arcade developer does not receive or control those copies or backups.

MIDI Arcade does not provide user accounts, so there is no server-side account or developer-held account data to delete.

Permissions and device access

MIDI Arcade requests access only when needed for a feature you start, such as connecting compatible MIDI hardware or opening Android's share sheet. The current Android app does not request access to your audio library, photos, contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and it does not declare the Android Internet permission.

Security

Local app data is protected by Android's application sandbox and your device's security controls. No storage method is perfectly secure, so protect your device and use care when sharing exported MIDI files.

Children's privacy

MIDI Arcade is a general music-creation tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

If MIDI Arcade later adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety form will be updated before that collection begins. Material changes will be shown through a revised effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the monitored developer contact address displayed under App support on MIDI Arcade's Google Play listing. Include “MIDI Arcade privacy” in the subject line.
